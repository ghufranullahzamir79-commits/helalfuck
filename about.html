Our Story

The art of less.

The maison began as a dressing table edited down to what a person actually finishes: a foundation, a lip, a blush, a short ritual for skin, two tools, and two pieces of jewelry that can be worn with all of it.

We work in nude, bronze, and espresso. Campaign photographs in this edit are the six rooms of the house: luxury cosmetics, care tools, everyday glow, Sara's jewelry, the storefront, and the glow ritual.

Our Philosophy

The Helalune codes.

01 / BEAUTY

Nude light

Color stays close to skin. If a shade shouts, it does not ship.

02 / DESIGN

Sharp cut

Packaging, type, and trays keep a right angle. Nothing is softened into a pill.

03 / JEWELRY

Honest metal

Hoops and the cuff are gold-tone imitation. They are made to be worn, not stored as treasure.
